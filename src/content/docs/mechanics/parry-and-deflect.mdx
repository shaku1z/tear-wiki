The current numeric parry and deflect thresholds are not included in the validated public reference; the table marks each legacy path accordingly.

A successful melee parry negates the incoming damage and creates a recovery window. Projectile deflection changes the projectile’s ownership and sends it away from the impact point; a perfect deflect unlocks the strongest upgrade interactions.
