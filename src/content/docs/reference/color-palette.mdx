The arena communicates through colour: aggression, counterplay, player feedback, hazards, and support roles all have their own signal. This swatch board is generated from the validated stage theme records in the public game reference. Global runtime color settings are outside that published contract.

<PaletteConsole />

## Reading the arena

- Warm signals generally announce immediate aggression or impact.
- Cool signals identify ranged pressure, movement, and high-precision counterplay.
- Player-facing effects form a consistent visual language across the blade, successful deflects, and perfect interactions.

Use the stage and enemy dossiers for the gameplay meaning behind each published theme color. Other runtime color settings are not published here.
