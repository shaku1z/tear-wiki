The **Meta Shop** is a persistent-progression concept in Tear. The current catalog, prices, level progression, and effects are not included in the validated public game reference, so the table below marks that boundary instead of presenting legacy values as current.

Unlike in-run drafting, Meta shop upgrades are intended as a separate progression layer. Current costs, currencies, and unlock requirements remain unpublished in the public reference.

## Design Philosophy

The intended meta shop design is described around three core principles:
1. **Not invalidate skill.** No meta upgrade makes you invincible. A maxed-out player can still die in seconds to a Boss if they fail their parries.
2. **Reward time invested.** The massive Coin costs of the final tiers are designed as prestige sinks for veteran players.
3. **Provide meaningful choices.** Do you want to be a glass cannon with maxed *Sharpness*, or a tank with maxed *Toughness* and *Warding*? The choice defines your optimal build path.
